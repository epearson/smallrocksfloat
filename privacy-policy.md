# Privacy Policy
Last updated: 5/7/2026

## What We Collect
This application collects phone numbers voluntarily provided by users for the purpose of sending SMS notifications.

## How We Use It
Phone numbers are used solely to deliver application notifications, alerts, and responses via SMS.

## Third Parties
Messages are delivered via Twilio. No personal data is sold or shared for marketing purposes.

## Data Retention
Phone numbers are stored only while the user's account is active.

## Opt Out
Users may stop receiving messages at any time by replying STOP or removing their number from the application.
